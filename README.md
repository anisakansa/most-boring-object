## The review

> The dustpan has one job — catch the dirt — and it leaves a thin grey line behind every single time.

99 characters, one line. The dustpan is the boring object nobody reviews, and its one unmistakable failure is the thin line of dust it leaves behind after every sweep — a detail anyone who has ever swept a floor recognises instantly. The turn comes early ("one job") and the last words sharpen it ("every single time").
